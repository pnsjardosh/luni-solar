Obliquity / Aksha Jhukav --

Earth's axial tilt produces the annual north-south motion of solar declination, the solstices, and the physical basis of Uttarayana and Dakshinayana. Sidereal Makara and Karka sankrantis occur later than tropical solstices because of ayanamsha.

How this is calculated

Gujarati New Year starts on Kartak Sud 1, the Shukla Pratipada immediately after the Diwali Amavasya new moon. This view finds that new moon near October-November, starts the year on the following civil day, then follows each next new-moon cycle to map Kartak through Aaso. Adhik months appear when a lunar month has no solar rashi ingress.
